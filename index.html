AI BUSINESS LAB

Original experiments. Measured results.

AI Business Lab is an owner-operated project for producing original educational videos about local AI and business automation. It combines original scripts, recorded software results, local speech synthesis, video rendering, captions and review workflows.

Private preparation, explicit publishing approval.

Videos are prepared and staged privately. Public release dates are activated only after the channel owner reviews the content and calendar.

What the application does

The local production application runs on the owner's Mac. Python and FFmpeg assemble videos and Shorts; local Piper produces narration; Ollama supports local drafting and experiments; n8n coordinates local workflow checks. Synthetic demonstration inputs are labelled. Drafts and technical checks are separate from final content approval.

Why it requests Google access

The owner authorizes the official Google OAuth flow to connect the designated AI Business Lab YouTube channel. The application uses YouTube permissions to upload video files, manage their captions and thumbnails, and set approved publication dates. Read-only YouTube Analytics access supplies aggregate channel reporting. It does not request Gmail, contacts or calendar access.
