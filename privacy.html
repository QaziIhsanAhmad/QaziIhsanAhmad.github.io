Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026.

This policy describes the owner-operated AI Business Lab video production application and this informational website. Project contact: xenovasolutionsofficial@gmail.com.

Google data accessed

With the owner's consent, the application obtains OAuth access and refresh tokens for the designated YouTube channel. It reads channel identity and status, video metadata and processing status, captions information when needed, and aggregate YouTube Analytics reports such as views, estimated watch time, average view duration and subscriber changes. It uploads the owner's video, caption and thumbnail files, manages related metadata, and assigns publication dates only after explicit final approval.

Purpose and limited use

Google user data is used only to connect the designated channel, prepare and manage the authorized video workflow, verify operation results, and provide owner-facing reports. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use Google channel or analytics data to train general-purpose AI models. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Local storage and access

OAuth credentials are stored in local credential files with access restricted to the local user account. Production scripts, media, metadata, upload ledgers, review records and reports are also stored on the owner's Mac. The local studio and worker use those records to produce and review content. Restricted file permissions are a protection measure; this policy does not claim that all local files are encrypted or that local execution eliminates every network interaction.

When data is shared

Authorized videos, metadata, captions and thumbnails are transmitted to Google/YouTube for channel operations. API requests and token refreshes are sent through the official Google interfaces. Videos remain private during staging; public visibility and publication dates require the owner's final approval. The application does not provide third-party advertisers with Google user data. The local speech and language-model stages operate with installed models; Google analytics records and OAuth secrets are not submitted to those models.

Retention and removal

Local credentials and records are retained for the owner's ongoing workflow until the owner deletes them or withdraws authorization. The owner may revoke access through Google Account permissions and request help removing local credentials or project records at the contact address above. Revoking access stops future authorized API use; it does not automatically delete videos already stored by YouTube. Such videos remain under the channel owner's control and YouTube's policies.

Website visitors

This static website has no application sign-in, visitor submission form, advertising script or application analytics script. GitHub Pages provides hosting and may process ordinary request information under GitHub's privacy statement. Following a YouTube or Google link subjects that visit to the destination provider's policies.

Security and policy updates

Do not send passwords, passkeys or verification codes to the project contact or insert them into review materials. Complete account security steps only on Google's official pages. Changes to the application's data practices will be reflected in this policy and local notices.
